Our Foundation

Three timeless principles have guided Freemasons for over 300 years.

Brotherly Love

Every Mason regards his fellow man as a brother, practicing tolerance and respect for every person regardless of their background.

Relief

We actively support our brothers, their families, and the wider community through charitable works and mutual aid.

Truth

We pursue moral and intellectual truth, striving to improve ourselves in mind, body, and spirit in service to others.

About the Lodge

Rooted in Pataskala Since 1868

Pataskala Lodge #404 has been a cornerstone of our community for over 150 years. Our members include neighbors, professionals, and leaders who share a commitment to making themselves — and the world around them — better.

We meet regularly to conduct lodge business, confer degrees, and strengthen the bonds of brotherhood. Visitors and prospective members are always welcome at our stated communications.

Giving Back

Charity & Community

Relief is one of the three great tenets of Freemasonry. For Pataskala Lodge #404, that is not a slogan — it is a commitment lived out in our community every year.

Our Commitment

More Than a Fraternity

Freemasonry has always held that a man's obligations do not end at the lodge room door. They extend to his family, his neighbors, and the broader community in which he lives. Pataskala Lodge #404 takes that obligation seriously — devoting time, talent, and treasure to causes that make Licking County a better place to live.

Our charitable work is guided by one simple principle: we give where we can make the most difference. That means supporting local organizations with direct community impact, contributing to statewide Masonic charities, and showing up — in person — when our neighbors need us most.

Where We Give

Our Charitable Focus Areas

From local food banks to children's hospitals, our giving spans the needs of our community.

Local Food Pantry Support

We make regular donations and volunteer with the Licking County food pantry network. Food insecurity in our county is real, and our Brothers show up with both supplies and labor to help families in need year-round.

Student Scholarships

Each year, Pataskala Lodge #404 awards scholarships to deserving high school students in the Pataskala area. We believe investing in education is one of the highest forms of Relief — it changes the trajectory of a life.

Ohio Masonic Home

We support the Ohio Masonic Home in Springfield, Ohio — providing care and housing for elderly Masons, their spouses, and widows. Our contributions help ensure that Brothers who gave a lifetime of service are cared for with dignity.

Shriners Hospitals for Children

Many of our Brothers are also Shriners, and the lodge supports the Shriners Hospitals network — which provides free orthopedic and burn care to children regardless of their family's ability to pay. It is Masonic charity at its finest.

Brother & Family Assistance

When a Brother or his family faces hardship — illness, job loss, or unexpected crisis — the lodge is there. Masonic relief is personal, discreet, and immediate. We take care of our own, as we always have.

Community Events & Service Days

From cleanup days to holiday meals, the lodge participates in community events throughout the year. We believe charity is not only financial — it is showing up, rolling up your sleeves, and making your town better.

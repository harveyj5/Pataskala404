Getting Here

Directions to the Lodge

Pataskala Lodge #404 is located in downtown Pataskala, just off the main street. Parking is available on the street and in nearby lots.

116 S. Vine Street
Pataskala, Ohio 43062

From Columbus: Take I-70 East to Exit 112 (Pataskala). Head north on Broad Street, then turn right on Vine Street. The lodge is on the left.

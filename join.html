Membership

How to Join Pataskala Lodge #404

Freemasonry begins with a sincere desire to better yourself and serve your community. If that describes you, we invite you to take the first step.

Before You Apply

Qualifications for Membership

To petition Pataskala Lodge #404 under the Grand Lodge of Ohio, a man must meet these requirements.

Born Male

Pataskala Lodge #404 is a male-only fraternal society. Per Grand Lodge of Ohio Landmarks, a petitioner must be born male and continue to live as a man.

At Least 18 Years Old

Petitioners must be of legal adult age (18 or older) in the state of Ohio at the time of their petition.

Belief in God

A petitioner must sincerely believe in the existence and perfection of God. Freemasonry is non-sectarian — all faiths are welcome provided this belief is genuine.

Good Moral Character

A petitioner must come of his own free will, uninfluenced by others and unbiased by improper motives. He must be willing to conform to the customs and Landmarks of the Fraternity.

Ohio Resident

You should have an established residence in Ohio, ideally in or near Pataskala / Licking County, so you can actively participate in lodge life and meetings.

Desire to Serve

Membership is most meaningful for those who sincerely wish to better themselves and be of service to their fellow creatures — not for personal gain or social advancement.

Grand Lodge of Ohio

Landmarks of the Order

Landmarks are universal customs and regulations — both written and unwritten — originally established at a time so remote that no account of their origins is to be found. They are considered essential to the preservation and integrity of the Order.

The Grand Lodge of Ohio Constitution requires that these Landmarks "be held inviolate." By petitioning Pataskala Lodge #404, you agree to conform to each of them.

  • ✦ Acknowledgement of a belief in God
  • ✦ Freemasonry makes Masons of men only (determined by sex at birth)
  • ✦ Secrecy — business meetings are private; member privacy is respected
  • ✦ Symbolism of operative Masonry
  • ✦ Three degrees: Entered Apprentice, Fellow Craft, and Master Mason
  • ✦ Purposes are charitable, benevolent, educational, and for the worship of God
  • ✦ Controversial politics and sectarian religion are excluded from all activities
  • ✦ The Sacred Book of Divine Law must be present in lodges while at work

Source: GLO Form 21 (2023), Page 2

After You Submit

What Happens Next

Here is what to expect after your petition is received by the lodge.

1

Confirmation

A Brother will contact you to confirm your petition was received and answer any initial questions.

→
2

Investigation Committee

Three lodge members will be appointed to meet with you in person and report back to the lodge.

→
3

Reading & Ballot

Your petition is read at a stated meeting. Members vote by secret ballot. A unanimous vote is required for acceptance.

→
4

Initiation

Upon election, you will be scheduled for your Entered Apprentice degree — the first step of your Masonic journey.
